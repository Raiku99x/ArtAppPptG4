Project Overview

Background and Rationale

Inspired by MOBA games

Inspired by team-based games such as League of Legends and their battlefield environments.

Battlefield and visual presentation

Focuses on a 3D map and loading screen to present the game setting and opposing teams.

Art in game design

Explores how colors, shapes, textures, and composition contribute to digital game visuals.

Project Goal

Connect artistic creativity with digital technology and game development.

The Rift, built in 3DLanes, jungle, river pits and two bases
Research Process

Data Gathering and Subject Overview

01 Research

Reviewed MOBA games and their visual characteristics to understand common design features.

02 Study

Examined map layouts, paths, bases, towers, jungle areas, and loading screen compositions.

03 References

Collected visual references to guide the planning and appearance of the proposed designs.

OUTCOMEThe research provided ideas for the battlefield environment and the loading screen's visual arrangement.
